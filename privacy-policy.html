Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the Instagram assistant operated by MRS Properties (Suresh) and @subhi_vlogss ("we", "us") handles information. The assistant automatically replies to comments and direct messages sent to our own Instagram professional accounts: @sureshkumar.kumar.92123 and @subhi_vlogss.

1. Information we access

  • Comments posted on our own Instagram posts and reels (text, username, time).
  • Direct messages that people send to our accounts (text, sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username, time).
  • Our own posts and reels (captions and video audio) so the assistant can answer questions about properties we have posted.
  • Basic account insights for our own accounts (reach, views, engagement).

We do not access any account other than our own, and we never send the first message to anyone. We only reply to people who contacted us.

2. How we use it

  • To generate and send a reply to the comment or message you sent us.
  • To understand the context of an ongoing conversation so the reply is relevant.
  • To answer property questions (area, price, size) using details from videos we posted.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not share it with third parties except as described below.

3. Service providers

Message and comment text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate the reply text. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and does not use API data to train its models. Data is exchanged with Meta through the official Instagram Graph API.

4. Storage and retention

The assistant runs on our own computer. It stores only message and comment IDs (to avoid replying twice) and property details extracted from our own videos. Message contents are not permanently stored by us; they remain in Instagram as normal. You can ask us to delete any stored data at any time.

5. Talking to a human

Replies are automated. Type HUMAN in a direct message at any time and the assistant will stop replying in that chat, and the account owner will respond personally.

6. Your rights and data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, or for any privacy question, email subhikshasuresh1701@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We will act within 7 days. You can also remove our app's access at any time in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites.

7. Children

This service is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
